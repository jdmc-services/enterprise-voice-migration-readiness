# Privacy and public-content rules

This repository is independently constructed from synthetic examples, the author's explicitly reported experience and listed public references. It is not an anonymized customer dataset. It contains no intended customer telephone numbers, employer identity, account records, invoices, internal configurations, credentials or personal contact details.

Use non-dialable IDs such as NUM-00001 for portfolio examples. Removing names from a real bill or export does not make it suitable for this repository. Do not upload real incident logs, recordings, customer designs, screenshots, location records or contracts. Share sensitive engagement information only through approved private channels with agreed access and retention.

If you discover a possible disclosure, do not quote the content in a public issue. Contact the repository owner through an appropriate private channel and identify the file path and concern without repeating sensitive values. A public commit can remain in history after deletion; stop publication while investigating.

The automated scan checks selected credential, telephone-number and email patterns and package integrity. It cannot establish confidentiality, provenance, legal compliance or the absence of every sensitive item. Review the preview before publishing. Public repositories and public profile links can be seen and copied by anyone; a private repository is limited to authorized access under GitHub's controls.
