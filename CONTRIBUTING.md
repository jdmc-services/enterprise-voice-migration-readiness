# Contributing

Use synthetic examples and public primary references only. Do not submit real customer or employer material, personally identifying records, credentials or provider-confidential terms. Explain which assumptions changed and update dependent counts, formulas and documents together.

Run [validation](scripts/validate-repository.ps1) and [publisher tests](tests/test-publishing.ps1). When intentionally changing package contents, regenerate [the manifest](scripts/update-manifest.ps1) and review the entire preview. A manifest is an integrity check, not a signed provenance certificate.

Keep vendor positioning neutral. Cite product/release-specific evidence where relevant. Distinguish proposed methods, fictional assumptions, author-reported experience and observed results. Do not convert a planning example into a compliance or delivery claim.
