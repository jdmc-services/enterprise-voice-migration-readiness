# Project charter — illustrative

**Purpose:** establish an approved service baseline and migrate authorized numbers and related services through evidence-based gates.

**Scope:** discover number ownership, billable services, dependencies and service criticality; evaluate providers; validate design; pilot; execute approved waves; reconcile billing and inventory; obtain operational acceptance. Number portability, service migration, and legacy disconnection are separate decisions.

**Exclusions:** live carrier orders, production configuration changes, contractual advice, legal certification, procurement commitments, and any real customer/employer information. Cloud adoption or replacement of every device is not a predetermined objective.

**Decision rights:** the customer owns scope, business acceptance, contracts and disconnection authorization. The project manager coordinates evidence, schedule, risks, and escalation. Engineering owns design and testing. The carrier owns its port acceptance and activation processes. Security reviews access and recording controls. Emergency-calling specialists and responsible customer owners confirm applicable requirements. The sponsor accepts residual business risk; acceptance cannot waive legal obligations.

**Assumed constraints:** approved change windows, accurate customer service records, authorized signatories, compatible connectivity, available technical staff, and carrier-agreed orders. No wave date is firm until prerequisites and provider commitments are confirmed.

**Acceptance:** all in-scope services reconciled; test results recorded by category; critical defects resolved; monitoring and support handover accepted; cost baseline and actual billing reconciled; old services disconnected only after dependency and contractual review. No silent acceptance from an unanswered email.
