# Illustrative decision log

These decisions are part of the fictional scenario. They are not attributed to the author's historical work.

| Decision | Rationale | Owner and evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start with 100 standard IDs | Validate a manageable pilot before scale-up | Customer change owner; representative test plan and carrier acceptance |
| Treat special services separately | Device and life-safety dependencies may not fit standard voice migration | Service owner; supported path and category-specific tests |
| Hold legacy disconnection | Remaining dependencies can survive number activation | Customer; reconciliation, contracts and business acceptance |
| Avoid automatic port reversal assumptions | Local configuration restoration cannot reverse provider transfer | Engineering/carrier; stage-specific recovery approval |
| Compare like-for-like commercial offers | Number counts do not determine licensed seats or total cost | Customer/finance; verified units, terms, exclusions and transition fees |

The project manager records options, decision owner, evidence, date, affected scope and follow-up. A decision log records accountability; it does not replace authorized customer approval.
