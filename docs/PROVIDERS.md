# Provider comparison without vendor favoritism

Use the same requirements and evidence window for each candidate. The weights below are illustrative; the customer approves weights before reviewing scored offers. No provider is scored or endorsed here.

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Service coverage and portability | 20 | Specific locations, service classes and accepted transfer process |
| Continuity and recovery | 20 | Resilience design, incident ownership and tested feasible contingencies |
| Technical interoperability | 20 | Exact version/configuration evidence and customer-specific tests |
| Emergency and special-service support | 20 | Applicable requirements and supported device/service paths |
| Commercial clarity | 10 | Comparable term, taxes, fees, recurring units, exit and transition costs |
| Operational support | 10 | Escalation ownership, support hours and measurable service commitments |

Score each criterion 0–5 from documented evidence. Weighted score = sum(weight × score / 5), maximum 100. Unverified claims remain unscored; do not treat a blank as zero. Mandatory emergency, security and critical-service gates are pass/fail and cannot be compensated for by a low price or high total score.

Lumen's public enterprise communication FAQ describes an intended exit from relevant enterprise voice/communications services, subject to obligations and future customer notices. It does not establish one shutdown date for every customer or a blanket exit from dedicated internet/network services. Confirm product, contract and notice details with the provider before presenting urgency to a prospect. See [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md).

Disclose any referral or commercial interest before recommendations. A referral relationship does not prove technical suitability. Keep independent requirements, evidence and customer decision rights visible.
