# RACI interpretation

[data/raci.csv](../data/raci.csv) assigns exactly one accountable role per activity. A = accountable, R = responsible, C = consulted, I = informed. A/R combines accountability with execution.

Customer owner is an umbrella role requiring named delegates in a real program, including emergency-calling, service and finance owners. The carrier entry represents the appropriate accountable provider for the accepted order; each provider remains responsible for its own obligations. Engineering accountability for design does not give authority to approve customer contracts or waive legal requirements. Sponsor acceptance concerns residual business risk only.

The project manager coordinates evidence and decisions. The RACI is fictional and does not claim the author held every role. Actual role assignments require agreement before the project begins.
