# Independent engagement boundaries

JDMc Services commercial consulting and any referral activity are separate from employment and nonprofit activities. This portfolio does not name an employer, use employer/client assets, or suggest that an employer or nonprofit sponsors commercial work.

Commercial work requires its own agreed scope, authorization, fees and confidentiality terms. A referral introduction is distinct from paid engineering, project delivery, procurement authority and ongoing support. Do not imply authority to bind a customer or provider.

No partnership, reseller status, OTG entitlement, commission rate or compensation agreement is asserted. Verify the relevant written agreement before describing a relationship or compensation. Disclose relevant referral interests before recommending options, and preserve the customer's ability to evaluate alternatives.

Do not use nonprofit contacts, branding, funding, beneficiary information or communications channels to imply commercial endorsement. No charity benefit or tax treatment is promised. Do not present employer experience as an employer-authorized case study.

Vendor names are descriptive references. No logos or affiliation marks are included. Actual product, contract and compatibility decisions remain with the authorized parties.
